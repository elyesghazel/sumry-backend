## What this service stores

Sumry is a personal finance app. To run it, this instance stores:

- **Account data:** your name, email address and a hash of your password (never the
  password itself).
- **Your finance data:** accounts, balances, transactions, categories, rules, budgets,
  goals and planned payments you enter or import.
- **Sign-in sessions:** one per signed-in device and the time of the last sign-in, so you
  can see and end sessions in Settings.
